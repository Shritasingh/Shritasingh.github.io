Shrita Singh

I'm an incoming student in the Master of Science in Robotics program at Carnegie Mellon University, where I plan to focus on robot learning, motion planning, and end-to-end approaches to robot behavior — with an eye toward both the research and entrepreneurial sides of the field.

At IIT Bombay, my research centered on data-driven modeling, adaptive model predictive control, optimal control, and system identification. This work was recognized by two awards, including the R.G. Manudhane Student Excellence Award and an Undergraduate Research Award.

Most recently I was a Behaviour Planning Engineer on the trucking autonomy team at Applied Intuition in Tokyo, and before that an AI Researcher at Honda R&D Tokyo, working on cooperative behaviour planning for Honda's next-generation self-driving vehicles.
