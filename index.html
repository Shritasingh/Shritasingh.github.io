Shrita Singh

I'm an incoming student in the Master of Science in Robotics program at Carnegie Mellon University, where I plan to focus on robot learning, motion planning, and end-to-end approaches to robot behavior — with an eye toward both the research and entrepreneurial sides of the field.

At IIT Bombay, my research centered on data-driven modeling, adaptive model predictive control, optimal control, and system identification. I worked with Prof. Ravi Banavar on building optimal control schemes for a plug-in hybrid electric vehicle, and received the Undergraduate Research Award for my preliminary work. I went on to design novel data driven system identification schemes for adaptive model predictive control under the guidance of Prof. Sachin Patwardhan. My schemes showed reliable performance on a variety of benchmark systems - from quadruple-tank systems to a portable temperature control lab, and my work received the R.G. Manudhane Student Excellence Award for outstanding Mtech thesis.

Most recently I was a Behaviour Planning Engineer on the trucking autonomy team at Applied Intuition in Tokyo, where I worked on building motion planning algorithms for autonomous trucking. I was previously an AI Researcher at Honda R&D Tokyo, working on cooperative behaviour planning for Honda's next-generation self-driving vehicles. I was also a part of the IIT Bombay Student Satellite Team, where I worked on attitude control algorithms for a CubeSat sponsored by the Indian Space Research Organisation.
