Shrita Singh

I'm an incoming student in the Master of Science in Robotics program at Carnegie Mellon University, where I plan to focus on robot learning, motion planning, world models, and end-to-end approaches to robot behavior — with an eye toward both the research and the applied, entrepreneurial sides of the field.

Most recently I was a Behaviour Planning Engineer on the trucking autonomy team at Applied Intuition in Tokyo, and before that an AI Researcher at Honda R&D Tokyo, working on cooperative behaviour planning for Honda's next-generation self-driving vehicles. I hold a dual degree from IIT Bombay, where my Master's thesis received the R.G. Manudhane Student Excellence Award.
